Values

Care for Others

  • Listen first - seek to understand before offering solutions.
  • Acknowledge how your actions and decisions affect others, both inside and outside the team.
  • Speak and act with respect - regardless of role, seniority, or situation.
  • Build trust through consistency: say what you mean, do what you say.

Take Ownership

  • Step in when something needs to move, even if it's not "your job".
  • Come with a proposed solution when you raise a problem.
  • Close the loop - follow up until things are truly done.
  • Hold yourself accountable to outcomes, not just effort.

Make it Better

  • Debrief after projects - what worked, what didn't, and why.
  • When the same problem repeats, build a system to prevent it.
  • Share what you've learned - good ideas grow when they spread.
  • Question how things are done today - even when they're working.
